# Code of Conduct

We adopt the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Be empathetic and respectful, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, focus on what is
best for the community, and take responsibility for mistakes. Harassment, sexualized attention,
trolling, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project. Report
abuse privately to the repository owner through the contact methods on their GitHub profile. The
maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and may correct, warn, temporarily ban, or
permanently ban behavior according to its impact and recurrence.
